\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

In this multi-station daily PM$_{10}$ benchmark, conventional error-based
eligibility and dynamic fidelity identified different sets of forecast
cells. Rule A admitted 277 cells, whereas Rule B admitted 8; 269 cells
changed status, and 101 cells met the formal discordance definition. The
threshold sensitivity analysis showed that the qualitative eligibility consequence
persisted across the evaluated neighbourhood around the primary cut-offs.

The methodological implication is bounded but transferable: environmental
model evaluation should make clear whether error improvement and
preservation of decision-relevant dynamics are separate requirements. The
observed percentages and eligibility counts belong to this pollutant,
station network, model set, horizon range, and validation design. They do
not establish a universal rate or mechanism for other forecasting systems.
