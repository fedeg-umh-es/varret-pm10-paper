\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

In the evaluated multi-station daily PM$_{10}$ benchmark, 277 of 595 cells satisfied conventional error-based eligibility, whereas 8 satisfied the same rule plus the stated dynamic-fidelity requirements. The 269 changed statuses represent 97.1\% of Rule-A eligible cells, and 101 cells met the stricter formal discordance definition.

The methodological implication is that persistence-relative error skill and dynamic fidelity should be reported as complementary eligibility dimensions. The conclusion is descriptive and conditional on the pollutant, stations, model families, horizons, validation design, and study-specific thresholds; it is not a universal claim about forecasting systems.
