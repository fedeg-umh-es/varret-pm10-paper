\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

In this multi-station daily PM$_{10}$ benchmark, conventional error-based
screening and dynamic fidelity identified different sets of forecast cells.
Here, eligibility denotes only passage of the stated post-evaluation
heuristic cell screen, not model ranking, top-1 selection, or operational
acceptance. Rule A retained 277 cells, whereas Rule B retained 8; 269 cells
changed status, and 101 cells met the formal discordance definition. The
threshold sensitivity analysis showed that this cell-level screening
consequence persisted across the evaluated neighbourhood around the primary
cut-offs.

The bounded methodological implication from this case is that analogous
forecast evaluations may need to report whether error improvement and
preservation of decision-relevant dynamics are being treated as separate
requirements. The observed percentages and cell-screen counts belong to this
pollutant, station network, model set, horizon range, and validation design.
They do not establish a universal rate, acceptance rule, operational benefit,
or mechanism for other forecasting systems.
