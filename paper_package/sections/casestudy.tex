\section{Methods: Empirical Benchmark}
\label{sec:casestudy}

\subsection{Data and study population}
The frozen empirical evidence comprises daily PM$_{10}$ series from 17 heterogeneous Spanish monitoring stations identified in the MITECO/EMEP metadata. The station metadata represents urban, traffic, industrial, suburban, and rural settings. The canonical evidence does not establish a single defensible calendar endpoint for the complete recovered station set; consequently, the manuscript reports the verified station coverage and daily resolution without inventing a period label.

The unit of analysis is a station--model--horizon cell. The complete grid contains 17 stations, five empirical model families, and seven horizons, giving 595 rows. Each row summarises matched out-of-sample diagnostics for one model at one station and horizon. Shared stations, time series, and model families mean that these rows are structured cells, not independent replicates.

\subsection{Forecast task and validation}
The task is direct multi-step daily point forecasting at horizons $h=1,\ldots,7$. Evaluation follows five expanding rolling-origin folds in chronological order. Information available after an origin is excluded from fitting, feature construction, preprocessing, threshold estimation, and verification inputs for that origin. Missing verification targets are excluded rather than fabricated. The frozen aggregate does not recover exact per-fold row counts or a single complete train/test calendar, so those details are not asserted.

\subsection{Leakage controls}
The protocol requires train-only treatment of quantities estimated within a split. This includes preprocessing statistics, event thresholds, and model fitting. P75 thresholds are estimated from the corresponding training window rather than from verification outcomes. Persistence is evaluated on the same matched forecast cases used for candidate-model comparisons. The available provenance supports these controls, but does not support additional claims about unrecorded feature or hyperparameter details.

\subsection{Model families}
The five empirical model labels are direct Histogram Gradient Boosting (HGB), direct Ridge regression, fixed-order SARIMA, seasonal naive, and STL+Ridge. HGB and Ridge are direct horizon-specific forecasters; SARIMA is a recursive statistical family; seasonal naive is a seasonal carry-forward reference; and STL+Ridge is a decomposition/regression hybrid. The verified and unresolved configuration details are consolidated in the model evaluation specification above.
