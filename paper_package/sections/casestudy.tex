\section{Empirical Benchmark}
\label{sec:casestudy}

The empirical evidence is the frozen table \texttt{outputs/tables/master\_diagnostic\_table.csv}, whose provenance identifies 17 heterogeneous Spanish MITECO/EMEP monitoring stations and daily PM$_{10}$ series. The canonical table contains seven horizons and five empirical model families. The source package is recovered evidence rather than a new data download; its provenance and known row-level limitations are recorded in \texttt{evidence/paper\_a/README.md}.

\subsection{Forecast task and models}
The task is multi-step daily point forecasting evaluated at horizons $h=1,\ldots,7$. The five model labels in the canonical evidence are direct Histogram Gradient Boosting (HGB), direct Ridge regression, fixed-order SARIMA, seasonal naive, and STL+Ridge. Persistence is the reference forecast used for skill and Diebold--Mariano comparisons; it is not counted among the five empirical model families.

The resulting unit of analysis is one station--model--horizon cell. The grid is complete: $17\times5\times7=595$ cells, with no duplicate keys. The cells summarise matched out-of-sample forecasts across the rolling-origin evaluation artefacts. They are not independent observations because cells share stations, time series, and model families.

\subsection{Validation and preprocessing controls}
The project protocol specifies rolling-origin evaluation with five expanding folds, daily frequency, a maximum horizon of seven days, persistence as the required baseline, and train-only treatment of quantities estimated within a split. P75 event thresholds are estimated from the corresponding training window. Missing verification targets are excluded rather than fabricated. Details not recoverable at the same level from the frozen aggregate are listed in \texttt{UNRESOLVED\_METHOD\_DETAILS.md}.
