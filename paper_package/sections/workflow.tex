\section{Evidence and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:workflow}

All reported counts and summaries are derived from the canonical 595-row table. Its SHA-256 is recorded in the provenance manifest. The table contains exactly five model labels. Reproducible table and figure generators are included in the package, and the numerical claim-to-source map is provided in the accompanying traceability file.

The audit is intentionally descriptive. Counts are reported over a structured station--model--horizon grid, and the shared stations and time series mean that the 595 cells must not be interpreted as 595 independent replicates. Historical synthetic or quarantined material is outside the empirical evidence used here.
