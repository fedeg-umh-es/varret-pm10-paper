\begin{abstract}
Error-based forecast evaluation can identify improvements over persistence without showing whether forecasts preserve dynamic PM$_{10}$ behaviour. We audit whether adding explicit dynamic-fidelity requirements changes model eligibility in a rolling-origin benchmark of daily PM$_{10}$ forecasts from 17 Spanish monitoring stations, five model families, and seven horizons. Rule A requires positive persistence-relative RMSE skill and a significant comparison with persistence; 277 of 595 cells satisfy it. Rule B adds variance retention and P75 exceedance-recall thresholds, leaving 8 eligible cells. Thus, 269 cells change status, corresponding to 97.1\% of Rule-A eligible cells. The result shows that error eligibility and dynamic fidelity provide complementary information in this benchmark. Thresholds are operational audit criteria rather than universal standards, and the structured cells are not independent replicates. The conclusion is bounded to the evaluated pollutant, stations, models, horizons, and validation design.
\end{abstract}
