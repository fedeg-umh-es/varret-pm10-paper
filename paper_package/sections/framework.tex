\section{Eligibility Audit Framework}
\label{sec:framework}

The analysis applies two post-evaluation filters to every station--model--horizon cell. They do not produce a final winner for each station and horizon; they quantify how many cells pass a conventional error screen and how many remain after adding dynamic-fidelity requirements.

\subsection{Error-based eligibility: Rule A}
\begin{equation}
\textbf{Rule A:}\qquad \mathrm{Skill}>0 \quad\mathrm{and}\quad \mathrm{DM}_{BH}\ \text{significant}.
\end{equation}
Skill is persistence-relative RMSE skill. The significance flag comes from a two-sided Harvey--Leybourne--Newbold modified Diebold--Mariano comparison with Benjamini--Hochberg adjustment. The multiplicity family is verified in the implementation as the valid model--horizon comparisons within each dataset; it is not one pooled family across all 595 cells \cite{diebold1995,harvey1997}.

\subsection{Fidelity-aware eligibility: Rule B}
\begin{equation}
\textbf{Rule B:}\qquad \text{Rule A}\quad\mathrm{and}\quad \alpha\geq0.50\quad\mathrm{and}\quad \mathrm{Recall}_{P75}\geq0.20.
\end{equation}
The thresholds are operational criteria for this audit. They are not estimated optima, universal standards, or regulatory recommendations. A changed cell satisfies Rule A but fails at least one Rule-B condition.

\subsection{Formal discordance}
A formal discordant cell satisfies Rule A, has $\mathrm{Recall}_{P75}\geq0.20$, and has $\alpha<0.50$. This definition is stricter than the two-dimensional region of positive skill and low alpha shown in Figure~\ref{fig:skill_alpha}; the latter does not itself include the DM and recall conditions.
