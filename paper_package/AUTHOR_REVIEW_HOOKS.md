# Author review hooks

These are targeted scientific review points, not requests to “humanise”
the prose.

| Location | Revised wording | Evidence constraint | Why author review matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction, final paragraph | “The contribution is the quantified decision audit and its bounded evaluation-design implication...” | No new metric or universal framework | Confirm that this is the intended contribution |
| Methods, data paragraph | “The recovered station package does not establish one defensible common calendar endpoint...” | Exact period not recoverable | Confirm the limitation is accurately stated |
| Methods, models paragraph | “Historical SARIMA records also conflict...” | `SARIMA_CONFIGURATION_CONFLICTING` | Confirm the wording matches the provenance record |
| Results, first paragraph | “These are eligibility counts ... not evidence that one model is the final winner...” | Rule A is a cell filter | Confirm the paper does not imply winner selection |
| Results, discordance paragraph | “The pooled ... association is descriptive...” | rho = -0.863 pooled | Confirm the clustering qualification is sufficient |
| Results, decision paragraph | “The joint evidence changes the decision set...” | 277 -> 8, 269, 97.1% | Confirm the central result is framed as eligibility |
| Discussion, limits paragraph | “97.1% is the proportion of Rule-A cells...” | Structured non-independent cells | Confirm the denominator and limitation are clear |
| Discussion, family paragraph | “without identifying a causal architectural mechanism” | No causal analysis | Confirm this reflects the authors’ interpretation |
| Discussion, transferability paragraph | “The transferable insight is methodological rather than empirical...” | Only the principle transfers | Confirm the statement is scientifically honest |
| Conclusions, second paragraph | “The methodological implication is bounded but transferable...” | No universal rate or mechanism | Confirm this is the intended closing claim |
