# Narrative before/after audit

## Introduction

**BEFORE**

The previous introduction presented the gap and contributions correctly,
but moved quickly from baseline-relative skill to the study question.

**AFTER**

The revised introduction moves from why environmental decisions need more
than average error, through the role and limits of RMSE and persistence,
to the decision problem, bounded EMS gap, and exact benchmark response.

**WHY THE REVISION IS BETTER**

It gives the reader a rationale before the question and distinguishes the
established metrics from the decision consequence being quantified.

## Results

**BEFORE**

“Rule A identifies 277 eligible cells...” was followed by compact metric
and figure blocks.

**AFTER**

“Rule A identifies a substantial set of cells that improve on
persistence,” followed by the family counts, the low-alpha contrast, the
formal cell-level discordance, and only then the 277-to-8 transition.

**WHY THE REVISION IS BETTER**

The strongest number is earned by the preceding evidence. The threshold
objection then has a natural place at the end of Results.

## Discussion

**BEFORE**

The previous Discussion used several one-paragraph micro-subsections that
read as a checklist of topics.

**AFTER**

The revised Discussion opens with the integrated decision consequence,
states the interpretive limits early, and then moves through complementary
metrics, family contrasts, literature, sensitivity, transferability, and
boundaries as one conversation.

**WHY THE REVISION IS BETTER**

Interpretation is connected to evidence and limitations rather than
presented as isolated headings.

## Conclusions

**BEFORE**

The previous conclusion was correct but compressed the observation and
methodological implication into a short summary.

**AFTER**

The revised conclusion states what differed, what changed in eligibility,
what evaluation principle follows, and which magnitudes remain benchmark-
specific.

**WHY THE REVISION IS BETTER**

The ending answers both the empirical question and the bounded
methodological question without introducing new results.
