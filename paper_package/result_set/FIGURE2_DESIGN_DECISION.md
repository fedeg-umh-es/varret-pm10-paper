# Figure 2 design decision

## Candidates

- **Option A:** five small multiples with identical limits, one panel per
  model family, black open contours for formal discordance, and a faint
  positive-skill/low-alpha region.
- **Option B:** pooled scatter plus a family summary of low-alpha and formal
  discordance proportions.

## Selection

Option A is the final design. It answers the scientific question more
directly because it shows where the 101 formal discordant cells occur
within each family while preserving identical axes for comparison. It
also makes the seasonal-naive and STL+Ridge regimes visible without
turning the pooled rho into a homogeneous relationship.

Option B is retained only as an inspected candidate in the result-set
working record and is not included in the manuscript or submission ZIP.

The shaded area denotes only positive skill with alpha below 0.50. The
open contours denote the stricter formal definition: Rule A, P75 recall
at least 0.20, and alpha below 0.50. No explanatory paragraph is placed
over the point cloud.
