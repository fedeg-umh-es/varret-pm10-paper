\section*{Data Availability Statement}
The frozen aggregate evidence and provenance manifest are included in the repository. The canonical diagnostic table contains 595 station--model--horizon rows and is accompanied by SHA-256 checksums. The evidence package identifies the series as recovered Spanish MITECO/EMEP monitoring-station data; the row-level manifest and known reconstruction limitations are documented alongside it.
