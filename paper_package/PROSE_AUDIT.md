# Prose audit

| Location | Problem | Original pattern | Revision action | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Too modular and list-like | Short context/gap/contribution blocks | Rebuilt as a funnel from decision importance to exact study response | Makes the problem and response continuous |
| Results | Telegram-like ordering | Separate mini-sections for each metric | Reduced to three result subsections and a staged argument | Builds error → fidelity → discordance → decision tension |
| Results | Figure-first openings | “Figure X shows...” as paragraph openings | Hallmark stated before the figure citation | Lets evidence follow the claim |
| Discussion | Checklist structure | Six one-paragraph micro-subsections | Rebuilt into two broad blocks and continuous paragraphs | Enables interpretation rather than enumeration |
| Discussion | Repeated generic transitions | “This result/supports/contribution” | Replaced with causal or logical links tied to the preceding evidence | Improves continuity and precision |
| Across paper | Empty adjectives | substantial, robust, important without numbers | Retained only when tied to the sensitivity range or replaced by counts | Keeps claims evidence-led |
| Across paper | Over-disclaimer risk | Repeated universal/optimal caveats | Consolidated boundaries while preserving necessary scope limits | Avoids defensive repetition |
| Claims | Framing drift | selection language | Uses eligibility filtering and decision audit | Matches the implemented operation |

No new literature search, result, anecdote, or AI-detection optimisation
was introduced.
