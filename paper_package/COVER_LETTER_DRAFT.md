# Cover letter draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Dynamic Fidelity and Model Eligibility
in Daily PM10 Forecasting: A Multi-Station Decision Audit,” for possible
publication in *Environmental Modelling & Software*.

The manuscript addresses an environmental model-evaluation problem:
error-based forecast skill and preservation of decision-relevant
dynamics answer different verification questions. We audit this issue
in a frozen rolling-origin benchmark of daily PM10 forecasts from 17
Spanish monitoring stations, five model families, and seven horizons.

The main result is a decision consequence rather than a claim for a new
metric. Of 277 cells eligible under the error-based Rule A, 8 remain when
the stated dynamic-fidelity requirements are added; 269 cells change
status (97.1% of Rule-A eligible cells). A deterministic sensitivity
audit shows that the qualitative consequence persists in the evaluated
threshold neighbourhood. These values describe this structured
benchmark and are not presented as population rates.

The paper fits the journal’s scope because it evaluates environmental
forecasting procedures, compares alternative model families under
temporal validation, documents quality-assurance controls, and makes a
bounded methodological point about treating error and dynamic fidelity
as distinct evaluation dimensions. The manuscript does not claim a new
universal metric or framework. A graphical abstract, if required, will
be created manually by the authors.

Sincerely,\
The authors
