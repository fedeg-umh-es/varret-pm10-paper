# Paper A — Title Options for Environmental Modelling & Software

**Option 1 (Canonical - Recommended):**  
*Variance Retention: A Forecast-Verification Diagnostic for Skilful-but-Smoothed Point Forecasts of Daily PM10*  
- **Character Count:** 105 characters  
- **Assessment:** Clear, defensive, leads with "Forecast-Verification Diagnostic" (verification framing), avoids overclaiming forecasting methodology or atmospheric mechanisms. Fully compliant with EM&S scope.

**Option 2 (Emphasizing Environmental Modelling):**  
*Variance Retention: Diagnosing Amplitude Collapse in Deterministic Point Forecasts of Daily PM10*  
- **Character Count:** 96 characters  
- **Assessment:** Highlights the diagnostic function ("Diagnosing Amplitude Collapse"), suitable for environmental software and modelling readers.

**Option 3 (Emphasizing Multi-Horizon Evaluation):**  
*Joint Evaluation of Skill and Dynamic Fidelity in Multi-Horizon Air-Quality Forecasting*  
- **Character Count:** 88 characters  
- **Assessment:** Broader evaluation framing, but less explicit on the specific variance retention diagnostic layer.
